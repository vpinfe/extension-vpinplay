# Sharing and accounts

VPinPlay keeps one account for each player who shares.  It is set up on **Players**, in a section named VPinPlay on the player's panel.  This page goes through it.

## No account yet

The section reads "No VPinPlay account", with two buttons.

- **Choose a User ID** picks the player's name on VPinPlay.
  - The field takes exactly what you type.  The dialog checks it after a short pause and shows **Available**, "Taken. If it's yours, use your card." or "Can't reach VPinPlay".
  - **Choose** works only for an available one, and the ID is saved in lowercase.  Nothing is sent yet.
- **Use a Card** takes an account from a card saved on another device.  This is how you carry an account to a second device, or recover your own after taking it off the first.

## Chosen, not shared

The section shows the **User ID**, a note that it is not on VPinPlay until you share, and the **Share** switch, off.  **Change User ID** picks a different name, and is free until the first time you share.

Turning **Share** on asks first.  The dialog lists what VPinPlay makes public, and says that the service cannot rename or delete an account.  The player needs initials, and the dialog says to add them in Details first if they have none.  Pressing **Share** claims the ID.  If that fails, because the ID was taken meanwhile or VPinPlay cannot be reached, you are told why and **Share** stays off.

What is sent is the ID and the player's initials, the games they play from now on with when and how long, and their ratings and high scores.

## Shared

Once claimed, the section has **Account** with:

- The **User ID**, and **Your Page**, a link to the public page on VPinPlay.
- **Share**, which switches sending on and off.  What was already sent stays with the service.
- **Waiting**, when games have not been sent yet, such as "3 games waiting", with **Send Now** to try again.  A line beside it says when the last games were sent.
- **Card**, a tile with the note that anyone holding it can play as you on VPinPlay, so keep it like a password.  **Show** makes it large, to scan with a mobile device.  **Save Card** saves it as an image.

## Taking an account to another device

Save the card from the first device with **Save Card**.  On the second, open the same player, press **Use a Card** in the VPinPlay section and choose the card file.  Replacing an account asks first, and offers **Save Card** for the one being replaced, because without its card that account can never be used again.

## Taking one away

- **Disconnect VPinPlay** is in the player's menu on **Players**.  It forgets the ID and the key on this device.  It asks first, and offers **Save Card**.  VPinPlay keeps what was shared, and only this device forgets.
- **Remove VPinPlay User ID** is in the same menu.  It is for an ID chosen but never shared.  Nothing was sent, so nothing remains.

Removing a player from **Players** deletes their account on this device too, so take the card first if the account matters.

## Keeping a game out

A game marked **Private** on **Games** is never sent to VPinPlay or any other community service.  **Mark Private** and **Mark Not Private** are in the game's menu.

## When something looks wrong

- **Share** will not turn on.  The player needs initials first, and the ID must be free on VPinPlay.
- "Can't reach VPinPlay" in the ID dialog.  The service or the internet is out of reach.  Nothing is lost, and you can choose again.
- Games are waiting and not going.  **Send Now** tries again.  If it fails, check **Server** on the extension's page under **System > Extensions**, which is blank unless you changed it.
- The card is lost.  Without it the account can never be used again, so save it before removing the player or disconnecting.
